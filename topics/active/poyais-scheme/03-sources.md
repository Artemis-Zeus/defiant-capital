## 来源卡片

### S001｜Rothschild Archive（档案介绍 + 1821 原信）

- 原文摘录（1821-06-30 MacGregor 致 Nathan Mayer Rothschild 信，档案号 XI/112/54）：
  > "I am at present forming a Settlement in the Kingdom of Poyais in South America, where I hold by Grant from the Indian King a Territory of about eight Millions of Acres, abundantly fertile, and remarkably healthy; but the Climate and Productions being Tropical, it is best calculated for Colonization by the Jewish Nation..."
- 中文翻译：
  > "我目前正在南美的 Poyais 王国建立一个定居点；依据当地印第安国王的授予，我在那里拥有一块约八百万英亩、极为肥沃且气候相当健康的领土；但由于气候与物产属热带，它最适合由犹太民族来殖民……"
- 精确定位：页面中部信件全文；档案参考号 XI/112/54 Sundry correspondence 'M', 1821。
- 其他关键摘录：
  - 蚊子国王授予（Sinclair 谱系口径）：页面称授予签署于 1820 年 4 月中旬、Cape Gracias a Dios（S002 转录的档案原件落款为 1820-04-29——两处日期口径不一，已登记 C009）。
  - 移民与幸存："Of the original 240 settlers who reached Poyais, only 60 survived the insanitary conditions, dysentery and fever, and 50 eventually returned to London, the others remaining in British Honduras."
  - 两船："Honduras Packet... departed from London... on 10 September 1822"（载约 70 人）；"Kennersley Castle... left Leith on 22 January 1823 with almost 200 settlers"。
  - 法国与委内瑞拉："Arrested, tried, and acquitted in France"；Josefa 1838-05-04 卒于爱丁堡；"died in 1845... buried with full military honours in Caracas Cathedral"。
- 支持主张：C002、C007、C008、C009、C012、C020、C021；反驳"移民全部无人生还"类极端说法。
- 局限：页面正文大量依据 Sinclair (1999) 叙事，属档案馆编辑介绍；日期与人数需与 S002/S007 口径表对照；"240/60/50"为该页单一口径。
- 独立性：档案馆原创介绍；信件原文为该馆保存档案的公布文本（一手）。
- 版权状态：网页未获完整归档授权，仅保存必要摘录。

### S002｜Clavel, Enterprise & Society 2021（ORA 全文预印本）

- 原文摘录（摘要首句）：
  > "This article revisits the story of one of the greatest financial frauds in history: Poyais. In the 1820s, Gregor MacGregor issued bonds for this alleged financial hub... he even sent several emigrant ships to its shores, which caused dozens of victims."
- 中文翻译：
  > "本文重新审视 Poyais——历史上最重大的金融骗局之一。1820 年代，Gregor MacGregor 为这个所谓的金融中心发行债券……他甚至把多艘移民船送去其海岸，造成数十名罹难者。"
- 精确定位：ORA 文件第 1 页摘要；全文 50 页可读。
- 关键段落摘录（均见该 PDF，含注释编号）：
  - 土地授予原文（fn33 引 Lloyds Banking Group Archives, 'Grant of Land by George Frederic', NRAS945/20/19/72, 29 Apr. 1820）："full power and authority to enact laws, establish customs... commonly called Black River, Polayas or Poyais. But let it be clearly understood, that there is nothing contained in this Deed, which shall be construed into a Cession of the Sovereignty of the Country as now held by His Mosquito Majesty."（授予范围 >33,000 km²）
  - 债券（"The Loan"节）：1822-10-23，John Perring（从男爵、议员、前伦敦市长，Perring, Shaw and Barber & Co. 合伙人）认购"Service of the State of Poyais" 6% 贷款，总额 £200,000；2,000 张 £100 面额无记名债券，发行价 £80，分期 £15/£35/£30；Lowe 佣金约 8%，Perring & Shaw 约 5%；Ogilvie 持有约三分之二。
  - 市场：1822-10-29 进入 Course of the Exchange；10 月底涨至 £86；分期两次展期下调；1823-05 Richardson 转换贷款（400 股经 Daniel Mocatta 以 5% 佣金售出）；1823-07 至 09 价格 £5—£20；行情表最后一次出现 1824-01-23；1824-11-09 Foreign Stock Market 委员会裁定 "non-cognizable"。
  - 殖民部知悉（RCSL GBR/0115/RCMS 270/46_41）：Belize 主管 George Arthur 1821-01-15 函告殖民部该授予，早于债券发行约一年半。
  - MacGregor 致财政大臣 Vansittart（1821-03-13, TNA CO 137/152）：欲在 Poyais 建"state"，"that may one day be useful to Jamaica"。
  - 国王撤销公告（TNA CO 123/34, 1823-03-28 转录）："he not having fulfilled his contract with me agreeable to his stipulations"。
  - 《泰晤士报》1823-06-09（fn105，Lloyd's 通讯）："these deluded creatures whom Sir GREGOR McGREGOR sent to the Masquito [sic] Shore [had] died miserably."；1823-09-01（fn107）："there is no Poyais sea or city of Poyais in existence, nor any appearance in that part of the country to warrant such an assertion."
  - 移民总数（fn113—114）："over two hundred individuals left in four ships"。
  - Strangeways 身份（fn82, TNA WO 25/775/8）：真实的第 65 团前军官——与 S007 庭审中 Picken"部分章节由 Sir Gregor 执笔"的证词并存（见 C022）。
  - 法国：1825-08-09 成立 La Compagnie de la Nouvelle-Neustrie（AN MC/ET/LX/673）；"Suspecting him of fraud, Parisian police incarcerated MacGregor for months... he would be declared innocent the next year"；1825-10-04 新债 Prospectus（BL）；1827-07-17 与 1831-09-10 后续券（NRS GD50/184/104/3）。
  - 委内瑞拉：1838 赴委、获抚恤；1845 去世；1839 年自述 Exposición（Caracas, 1839）。
  - 背景（引 Dawson 1990）：1822 年哥伦比亚/秘鲁/智利三笔贷款合计约 £4.2M；至 1825 年另有约 £20M；1826 年除巴西外全部违约。
- 支持主张：C001、C002、C004、C006、C007、C008、C009、C010、C011、C012、C015、C018、C019、C020、C021、C022、C024。
- 局限：单一作者研究；个别档案日期（如 Hansard 辩论"4 Apr. 1824, col.728"）与官方在线版（1824-03-04）不一致，逐条以官方记录为准；摘要"数十名罹难者"为概数口径，具体见 C012/C014 口径表。
- 独立性：独立学术研究；与 S001（Sinclair 谱系）、S006（1952 报道）、S007（同期法律报道）互为独立。
- 版权状态：现代论文，未获仓库完整归档授权；ORA 公开预印本仅保存链接与必要摘录。

### S003｜1822 年宣传册 Sketch（全文深挖）

- 原文摘录（献词页）：
  > "TO HIS HIGHNESS GREGOR, CAZIQUE OF POYAIS, THIS SKETCH OF THE [MOSQUITO SHORE] INCLUDING THE TERRITORY OF POYAIS, IS RESPECTFULLY INSCRIBED"
- 精确定位：LOC/archive.org 扫描，标题页与献词页；Edinburgh: William Blackwood / London: T. Cadell, 1822；W. Reid, Leith 印刷；约 400 页八开本。
- 关键摘录：
  - 序言自述："he has endeavoured, as much as possible, to avoid making any statement which might appear doubtful or exaggerated"（作者自称避免任何可能显得可疑或夸张的陈述）。
  - Cazique 告示（书内转录，"dated at Rio Seco the 13th of April 1821"）：赴欧目的为"procuring religious and moral instructors, the implements of husbandry, and persons to guide and assist in the cultivation of the soil"；"no person but the honest and industrious, shall find an asylum in the Territory"。
  - 土地定价敕令（书内引 "a decree dated 3d April 1821"）："The price of lands in the territory of Poyais is... fixed... at one shilling per acre, together with a feu-duty of one cent of a dollar per acre, payable after five years... but it is probable, that before six months, their price will have risen to at least five shillings an acre"（1 先令/英亩 + 5 年后起征年租，并明示半年内将涨到 5 先令——制造涨价紧迫感）。
  - 体量话术：Mosquito Shore 面积"one-third larger than the kingdom of Portugal"，约 70,000 平方英里，另加 Talamancas 约 4,200 平方英里。
  - 农业承诺："In the territory of Poyais, there are three crops of Indian corn in the year, and the produce will generally exceed one hundred bushels an acre."
  - 贸易承诺："manufactured goods to the value of upwards of fifty thousand pounds pass annually into the Spanish American provinces, through this territory alone"。
  - 劳动力承诺："aided as he will readily be, by the labour of a faithful, docile, and peaceable native population."
  - 荣誉体系：书中出现"Vice-Cazique of Poyais"（1820 年授予）与"Military order of the Green Cross"（1819 年授予）等头衔；作者署名"Thomas Strangeways, K.G.C. Capt. 1st Native Poyer Regiment, and Aid-de-Camp to His Highness Gregor"。
  - 苏格兰王统叙事："the chief of this very old clan... is directly decended from the ancient Kings of Scotland... the right of His Highness Gregor... to the Chieftainship, cannot be disproved."
  - 卷末插页广告：含"Cazique 肖像—国家地图—海岸视图—Black River 港口视图"四件插图（视觉素材 V005 线索）。
- 支持主张：C003、C010（配套宣传）、C022、C024；同时反驳"宣传册纯属凭空捏造地名"的简化（书中大量内容改编自真实早期文献）。
- 局限：宣传材料只能证明"对外宣称了什么"，不能证明描述为真；书中大量段落改写自 Henderson、Bryan Edwards、Wright 等前人著作（书内脚注可见）；"St. Joseph's"在书中仅见于编者注。
- 独立性：一手原件；与 S002 为被引关系。
- 版权状态：1822 年原作公有领域；LOC/Internet Archive 扫描仅链接引用。

### S004｜Hastie 第一人称记录（全文深挖）

- 精确定位：archive.org 全文；扫描藏方 John Carter Brown Library（Brown University，Louisa D. Sharpe Metcalf Fund 购藏）——出处信息本轮补录。
- 关键摘录：
  - 启航："the Kennersley Castle finally sailed from Leith Roads, with about 170 passengers, on the 22d January 1823"（此前因风暴两度折返）；"Most of them were engaged to serve Sir Gregor McGregor for twelve months from the time of their arrival"。
  - 雇佣条款：Hastie 本人"£20 of wages the first year, and £25 the second"，携眷船费 £30 从工资中扣；"We knew that there was fully twelve months provisions... on board"。
  - 船上预期："we should find a town, with a good many inhabitants... a young man, who was a cabin passenger... told us there was a government-house erected, and a theatre, of which he was going out to be the manager!!"（附作者等人宣誓书，见卷末 affidavit）。
  - 先行者："about sixty passengers, under Governor Hall, had sailed from London some months before us"（乘 Honduras Packet）。
  - 登陆（1823-03-22）："we were very much disappointed to find no town, houses, nor people, except a part of the former settlers, some of them still in tents, and some in houses or huts, made of bamboo, and thatched with a kind of reeds"——注意：并非"荒无人烟"，现场有先行定居者。
  - 后勤与卫生：Hall 返船只带回两桶朗姆酒；"the water, which we got out of the sand, the badness of which was one great cause of the sickness"；"The medicine-chest was said to have run dry, although... a full supply for 300 men for twelve months had been sent out with us"；Honduras Packet 从此未归（"turned pirate"传闻"perhaps mere nonsense"）。
  - 现场死亡（Kennersley Castle 批次）："The whole deaths... during our whole stay at Poyais, was three children (one of them my own) and five men, besides one who in a state of derangement shot himself"；"The whole number sick, when they embarked for Belize, did not exceed 30 or 40 persons."
  - 转机（Belize 方）：双桅帆船 Mexican Eagle 自 Belize 抵达（载给蚊子国王的礼物）；"Mr. Marshal Bennet, the chief magistrate and principal merchant at Belize, and Mr. Westby his clerk"登岸告知"as we had not got our wages we were free men"；"if we staid where we were, we would be sure to die"。
  - 国王撤销："Colonel Hall, with the Mosquito king, and some old men whom he called his counsellors, arrived... the king told us that he had revoked his grant to Sir Gregor, and that we must either take an oath of allegiance to him or leave the country; failing which, he would bring down a strong force and massacre every man of us."
  - 后续："I embarked in the above schooner with about 60 others"；Belize 医院"completely surrounded by stagnant water, emitting... a most pestilential smell"；"a great many people would have been saved, who afterwards miserably perished in the hospitals of Belize"；同船的 Archibald Handyside"escaped sickness until his arrival at Belize, where he died"。
- 支持主张：C005、C006、C007、C012、C013、C014、C015、C016。
- 局限：幸存者个人叙述（1823 年出版，带为自身与同伴辩护的立场）；"bring down a strong force and massacre"为转述国王语，语气可能放大；人数为其单船口径。
- 独立性：一手第一人称；与 S007 庭审证人（同批移民）部分重合但为独立叙述。
- 版权状态：1823 年原作公有领域。

### S007｜Annual Register Vol. LXVI（1824）"Law Cases"：McGregor v Thwaites 庭审全记录（本轮最重收获）

- 精确定位：https://archive.org/details/s25id13212210 （全文文本）；"LAW CASES, &c." 部分星标页码 18*—23*；案由：Morning Herald 两业主被诉诽谤；涉案文章刊于 1823-10-14 Morning Herald。
- 关键摘录：
  - Mansion House 程序（1823-10-13，文章全文转录）：Mr. Prince（common-council-man）与 Captain Joshua Antrim（ship Lloyd's）求见 Lord Mayor Elect，处置三名孤儿——其父母 Thomas Chalmers 夫妇自洪都拉斯搭船、途中死于热病；"the remnant of whom had sought the protection of the British authorities at Honduras"；移民曾被送往"the desart swamp"。
  - Codd 证明书原文（1823-07-29，Government House, Belize）："Whereas a considerable number of British subjects have been brought into this settlement from the Mosquito shore who are unable to procure a livelihood, and are destitute of the means to procure a passage to their native country..."（署 Edward Codd, major-general superintendant and commandant）。
  - 当庭指控（Prince 转述，属"其声称"）："above 200 of the victims of delusion had returned from the Mosquito shore to Honduras in a state of utter destitution and of disease, which terminated the sufferings of a great part of them soon after"；并称 MacGregor 诱使移民交出积蓄（各 £15—£30），换取"his bankers at Poyais"的汇票，而移民"Poyais... found that no such thing as a banking-house was in existence"。
  - 诉状：损害赔偿额定为 £2,000；被告抗辩四项（报道属实等）。
  - 证人 Edward Irving（爱丁堡土地代理）："In the whole, from 270 to 280 went on the voyage"；"I printed the notes of the Poyais Bank, at Edinburgh; there were many thousands of them struck off and sent to Poyais... not 70,000"；经手卖地款"several hundred pounds... not 1,000/"；其推介"were founded on published works. Bryan Edwards's book, and Wright's [Memoir of the] Mosquito Shore"；"I was to be a Knight of the Green Cross... there was a dress-coat of a green colour... The buttons had a coronet, and G. signifying Gregor"；"My office still continues; but I have sold no more, since we heard of the failure of the settlement."
  - 证人 Robert Swan："I sailed in the Honduras Packet, on the 10th of December 1822, for the Black River, Mosquito Shore. She carried out upwards of 50 emigrants."；"The settlers were afflicted with fever and ague. I found the chief town to be only two houses, made of bamboes, with doors of cedar."
  - 证人 Ann Bagster（very sickly condition）："I went out to Poyais in January, last year, with my husband and six children, in the Kinnersley Castle. My husband was a mason; he bought 40 acres of land for £8, and paid the money at the office; he received no order on the bank. When he arrived there was no land."；"there was no town, no covering, nothing but the bushes, and the moonshine above us."；"My husband and two children died in four weeks at Belize, of fever and ague. Above 100 of the settlers, out of the 150 who went out, died of the same disorder at the Black River, or Belize."
  - 证人 Andrew Picken：被许诺"cornetcy of lancers"；受命写"Lines to Poyais"；其妹写歌"The Poyais Emigrant"，"the ballad was to be hawked through the streets to attract the vulgar"；"A part of the book, purporting to be written by Mr. Strangeways, was written by sir Gregor, the nominal author being then in Jamaica"；剧院之谈"merely in jest, uttered over wine; but it conveyed a serious impression"；抵达后"no churches, no town, two huts belonging to an American, and a hut and stone house in ruins"；"The king of the country came to me, and prevented the settlers from taking land as the subjects of M'Gregor. He said, a grant had been made; but some stipulation not being fulfilled, he chose to consider it null and void."；"there were to be three legislative houses in Poyais... yeomen... lower house: and there were to be barons."；"A very shattered remnant only have come home."
  - 证人 Edward Lowe（移民）：出发前被告知"there was a town, public buildings going on, a colonel Woodbine in command of some men"；1823-01-30 抵达"no town"；约一个月后被选为代表团成员觐见"George Frederick Augustus, king of the Mosquito nation"："He declared that a grant had been given to M'Gregor, but, the conditions not being fulfilled, he held it null and void. He said we might remain, but must be in subjection to him, and renounce sir Gregor M'Gregor; and that no more should land without his permission."
  - 被告律师 Scarlett 结辩（可引为修辞）："a sovereign without a subject;... a land-agent without an acre to sell; captains, without soldiers; notes, without a bank or funds; and a theatre in the midst of the desert!"
  - 首席法官总结："in part, they were proved — that the emigrants were deluded — that they came home in distress or died abroad — and that their hopes were blasted. But the allegation imputing to the plaintiff that he fraudulently gave Poyais notes in exchange for money, did not appear to be proved."
  - 判决："The Jury... found a verdict for the defendants on the pleas, stating that the report was faithful; and for the plaintiff, on the pleas setting forth, that the matters charged therein were true, with 1s. damages."
