### S001｜SEC 1940 调查总结（ASR 19）

- 身份：Securities Exchange Act Release No. 2707 / Accounting Series Release No. 19，1940-12-05 发布；为 501 页全本报告（Section 21(a) 调查）的总结。全本报告 1940 年由 GPO 出版，可购（当时定价 60 美分）。
- 听证程序（第 2—3 页）：> “hearings commenced on January 5, 1939 and continued, with some necessary adjournments, through April 25, 1939.” 46 名证人：PW 合伙人及员工 9 人、其他所专家会计师 12 人、Controllers Institute 1 人、American Institute of Consulting Engineers 1 人、信托人会计师（S. D. Leidesdorf & Co.）2 人、伪造文件制作者 1 人、McKesson 员工 8 人、董事 11 人、委员会调查员 1 人。记录共 4,587 页证词、285 件证据（逾 3,000 页）。听证令日期 1938-12-29。
- 金额与构成（第 3—4 页，A. SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL FACTS）：1937-12-31（欺诈披露前最后一个年报日）经 PW 认证的合并总资产超过 8,700 万美元，其中约 1,900 万美元"now known to have been entirely fictitious"——存货 1,000 万、应收账款 900 万、银行存款现金 75,000 美元。> “For the year 1937, fictitious sales in these units amounted to $18,247,020.60 on which fictitious gross profit of $1,801,390.60 was recorded. At the time of the exposure of the fraud on or about December 5, 1938 the fictitious assets had increased to approximately $21,000,000.”
- 造假主体（第 4 页）：> “The fraud was engineered by Frank Donald Coster, president of McKesson & Robbins since its merger with Girard & Co., Inc. in November 1926. In reality Coster was Philip M. Musica who, under the latter name, had been convicted of commercial frauds.” 三兄弟：George E. Dietrich（真名 George Musica，助理财务主管）、Robert J. Dietrich（真名 Robert Musica，Bridgeport 收发货与仓储负责人）、George Vernard（真名 Arthur Musica，打理空壳公司的办公室、地址、银行账户）。
- 交易机制（第 4—5 页）：虚构采购来自五家加拿大供应商（"retained the merchandise at their warehouses"）；虚构销售经 W. W. Smith & Company, Inc.；收付款经蒙特利尔银行行号 Manning & Company；印刷信头的发票、通知单，以及与 Smith/Manning 的合同、担保和伪造的 Smith 信用报告。"The fictitious transactions originated early in the life of Girard & Co., Inc., Coster's predecessor concern, incorporated on January 31, 1923"。1935 年中起沿用该手法；此前货物"名义上经 Bridgeport 工厂收货再转运"；1931 年前对国内供应商做真实现金付款并大多以虚构销售回款收回。信托人会计师报告：实际现金净流出累计 24,777,851.90 美元，除 2,869,482.95 美元外全部回流。
- 审计程序结论（B. SUMMARY OF CONCLUSIONS）：现金工作 "conformed in scope to the then generally accepted standards of the profession"；应收账款程序 "conformed to then generally accepted procedures…although confirmation of the accounts was not included in the program"，并引行业随后采纳的函证要求 "wherever practicable and reasonable, and where the aggregate amount of notes and accounts receivable represents a significant proportion of the current assets or of the total assets of a concern"；存货方面 > "Meticulous verification of the inventory was not needed in this case to discover the fraud. We are not satisfied, therefore, that even under Price, Waterhouse & Co.'s views other accountants would condone their failure to make inquiries of the employees who actually took the inventory and to determine by inspection whether there was an inventory as represented by the client."
- 损益分析与合伙人复核（第 8—9 页）：> "such analyses of profit and loss accounts as were made were applied to improper combinations of departments with the result that significant relationships were concealed"；> "the partner in charge in this case was not sufficiently familiar with the business practices of the industry in question and was not sufficiently concerned with the basic problems of internal check and control"。
- 批发业务部分（第 8—9 页）：> "covering for 1937 approximately 70% of the reported assets and 85% of the net sales, and which occupied approximately 97% of the auditors' time, it appears that the work in these other units was carried out in a thorough fashion in accordance with generally accepted auditing practice"。
- 总结论（第 10—11 页）：> "the audits performed by Price, Waterhouse & Co. substantially conformed, in form, as to the scope and procedures employed, to what was generally considered mandatory during the period of the Girard-McKesson engagements. Their failure to discover the gross overstatement of assets and of earnings is attributable to the manner in which the audit work was done. In carrying out the work they failed to employ that degree of vigilance, inquisitiveness, and analysis of the evidence available that is necessary in a professional undertaking…"；> "the overstatement should have been disclosed if the auditors had corroborated the Company's records by actual observation and independent confirmation through procedures involving regular inspection of inventories and confirmation of accounts receivable, audit steps which, although considered better practice and used by many accountants, were not considered mandatory by the profession prior to our hearings."
- PW 抗辩（第 10—11 页）：> "Price, Waterhouse & Co. maintain that a balance sheet examination is not intended and cannot be expected to detect a falsification of records concealing an inflation of assets and of earnings if accomplished by a widespread conspiracy carried on by the president of a corporation, aided by others within and without the recognized ranks of a corporation's operating personnel…" SEC 回应：同类虚构单据的详细审计"merely would have covered a larger volume of the same kinds of fictitious documents"。
- 治理建议（第 5—6 页）：股东年会选举审计师并立即通知；由非高管董事组成委员会安排委托细节；意见书致股东、其他报告致董事会并送每位董事；审计师出席股东大会；未完成委托时出具工作范围与未完成原因报告。
- 局限：OCR 工作稿存在个别行脱落（如第 7—8 页存货段、McKesson 员工对存货的看法一句）；引用必须回看页面图像。英文摘录按原文核对，中文为忠实翻译。
- 版权状态：美国政府公文（公有领域）；扫描件由 SEC Historical Society 托管，引用时标明托管来源。

### S001-全本｜发现过程与刑事程序（Section II、Section III 节选，同一出版物全本扫描）

- 载体：GPO 1940 年版 501 页报告的 Section II（印刷页 13—21）与 Section III（含印刷页 128—131 Thompson 证词）、Appendix A（印刷页 447—），由 SEC Historical Society 主站托管扫描（2026-08-31 从旧站 demo/test 子域迁移至主站链接）。
- 起诉与接管（Section II 第 13—14 页）：> "The complaint was filed in the United States District Court at Hartford, Conn., on Monday afternoon, December 5, 1938, by Vincent W. Dennis, Corporation Counsel of that city, as a stockholder suing on behalf of himself and on behalf of all other stockholders of McKesson & Robbins, Incorporated"。诉状称虚构存货与应收"aggregated in excess of $10,000,000"。法官 Edwin S. Thomas 随即任命 Thomas J. Spellacy（哈特福德市长）与 Abram S. Weissman（纽黑文律师）为临时接管人。股票链：12-03（周六）上午 NYSE 券商自 Mrs. F. Donald Coster 账户交割 2,000 股给 Robert J. Dietrich；当天下午 New Haven 律师 Benjamin Slade（自称代表公司某未披露高管）交给 Spellacy；周一早上 Spellacy 交给 Dennis。
- 12-05 当晚（第 14—15 页）：Sullivan & Cromwell 合伙人兼董事 Wilbur L. Cummings 电话告知 Sidney Weinberg（Goldman Sachs 合伙人、McKesson 董事、NYSE 理事）；Weinberg 报告给当时在 Waldorf Astoria 出席晚宴的 John M. Hancock（Stock List 委员会主席）。当晚 Weinberg 公寓会议：Charles F. Michaels（执行副总裁）、William J. Murray, Jr.（第一副总裁）、Julian F. Thompson（财务主管）、Cummings、Weinberg 出席。Weinberg 证词：> "Mr. Thompson during the course of the evening outlined certain suspicions that he had as to what appeared to him to be fraudulent transactions in the crude drug department"。Weinberg 连夜致电 PW 高级合伙人 George O. May，May 即到会并答应次日与一名合伙人及该项目负责人到公司。
- 12-06（第 15—16 页）：周二开盘 NYSE 暂停交易；11:30 理事会表决无限期暂停。当时挂牌：1,583.4 万美元 20 年期 5½% 可转换债券（1950-05-01 到期）、605,964 股 $3 优先股、1,282,983 股 $5 面值普通股。同日 Cummings 致电 SEC 主席 William O. Douglas，"told him we'd welcome an investigation"；当天下午 SEC 发出调查令。注脚 10：证券于 1940-03-20 开盘恢复交易（来源：NYSE 上市申请补遗，1940-03-22）。
- 12-08 至 12-09（第 16—17 页）：12-08（周四）Coxe 法官批准 Chapter X 重整申请，任命 William J. Wardall 与 Charles F. Michaels 为信托人，同日准许 SEC 介入。12-09（周五）晨 SEC 将案件移交司法部；当天中午宣布将由美国纽约南区联邦检察官主持大陪审团审理。
- 身份确认与前科（第 18—19 页）：12-13 申诉状、Garrett W. Cotter 专员签发逮捕令；12-14 逮捕 Coster 与 George Dietrich（首次取得其照片与指纹）；次日晚在纽约 Sheriff Street 警察局旧档找到 Philip Musica 指纹，刑事鉴定局局长 Joseph J. Donovan 宣布比对证明 Coster 即 Philip M. Musica，> "notorious swindler who had twice before pleaded guilty to commercial frauds"。1909 年：> "he pleaded guilty to having bribed a customs official to mark down weights on cheese imports"，判 1 年监禁并罚 5,000 美元，不到 6 个月获总统赦免。1913 年：> "he pleaded guilty to having committed grand larceny in connection with the affairs of the United States Hair Company, in which faked shipments and documents on human hair are estimated to have involved 22 banks in a loss of over $1,000,000"；Musica 一家在新奥尔良开往洪都拉斯的船上被捕，Philip 独自担责，刑期缓 3 年后中止，"apparently in return for aiding the authorities in investigatory work"；后受雇为私人调查员，1920 年因 Barnett Baff"chicken murder"案被诉唆使伪证，至 1929 年（已以 Coster 身份执掌公司后）保释解除、起诉撤销。
- 起诉与死亡（第 18—20 页）：12-15 联邦大陪审团起诉 F. Donald Coster、George E. Dietrich、George Vernard 及 McKesson & Robbins, Incorporated，罪名违反 1934 年证券交易法第 13、32 条并共谋。> "It was at noon of the following day, Friday, December 16, after his past career had been exposed and just as the United States Marshal reached his home to rearrest him under this indictment, that Philip Musica, alias F. Donald Coster committed suicide." 12-19：纽约县大陪审团以重大盗窃与伪造罪起诉三兄弟（州案）；同日 Michaels 辞任共同信托人并由董事会选为总裁接替 Coster（报告注脚引 NYT 1938-12-20）；SEC 转入审计实务调查。12-22：联邦大陪审团以真名（George Musica、Arthur Musica、Robert Musica，化名为别名）再起诉三兄弟，三人后均认罪。1939-03-30：第三次联邦起诉，除三兄弟外列名 [Leonard] Jenkins、John H. McGloon、Horace B. Merwin、Rowley W. [Phillips]（邮件欺诈＋第 13、32 条＋共谋）。Merwin、Phillips 被陪审团全部无罪；McGloon（1928 年起任 comptroller）就共谋、邮件欺诈及 1935/1936 年报相关罪名无罪，就 1937 年报（欺诈披露前最后一份）一项被判有罪，上诉待决。
- 判决（第 20 页注脚）：1940-05-22 Grover M. Moscowitz 法官宣判——George Dietrich 2 年 6 个月；George Vernard 3 年；Robert Dietrich 1 年 6 个月；Benjamin Simon 3 年；John Jenkins 1 年 1 天；McGloon 1 年 1 天并处罚金（OCR 两轮读取 $5,000/$6,000 冲突，引用前须回看原页）；Leonard Jenkins 缓刑。
- Thompson 证词（Section III 印刷页 128—131，引听证记录页 R. 2067—2084 等）：1937 年 4 月经营委员会决定 4 个月削减存货 400 万美元（康涅狄格分部 100 万、酒类 200 万、批发药品 100 万）；> "As the months passed, I observed that the crude drug department was going up steadily in inventory instead of coming down." 1937 年末至 1938 年 1—2 月多次向 Coster 提出；Coster 归咎于"Herman and Stebe"超买——> "I knew was a lie because I knew they did not have anything to do with that department." Thompson 于是自行查证：抄下加拿大仓库名址、重看 Smith 合同、到中央车站查蒙特利尔电话簿（加拿大册上只有 W. W. Smith and Company, Limited，"not the company that was tied in with the contract"）；托大学室友（一家清漆公司总裁）调 Dun 报告——> "The Dun report on Smith is dated, I think, the 28th of February, 1938"；> "I left out completely the forged Dun reports which I also got hold of early in the Spring from our files"；其律师合伙人 Prentice 查到纽约公司 W. W. Smith 约 1936 年已解散；1938 年秋 Prentice 发现 W. W. Smith 在布鲁克林电话簿上，派人查访"discovered the office of Vernard"。查付款环节时因该分部注销支票存在 George Dietrich 办公室，为避免惊动，Thompson 直接去 Guaranty Trust 请求在支票送返 Bridgeport 前查看。> "I think it was about the 16th of October." —— 约 10-16 首次将怀疑告知 Michaels；证词确认"October 16 is the first time you stated to anybody in the company anything concerning your suspicions…That's correct." Michaels 愿自费聘请 Downey 律师（"he said he would pay for Mr. Downey"），并认同 > "the important thing was to get something tangible, that this was all negative information and not positive"。
- 补充摘录（Section II/III 其他页）：
  - 诉状金额（Section II 第 13 页）：> "the fictitious inventories and accounts receivable aggregated in excess of $10,000,000"；法官会见（第 14 页）：> "On Sunday, Weissman…consulted with the Judge about a conference he had had with George E. Dietrich, assistant treasurer of the Corporation."
  - 曼宁决议（Section III 第 115 页附近）：> "at a meeting of the Board of Directors of the Canadian Company, held on April 4, 1935, it was resolved: 'That Manning & Co. of Montreal, Canada and branch of Manning & Co. in New York be and they hereby are appointed bank depositaries for McKesson & Robbins, Limited'…"。
  - 询价仪式（Section III）：> "The first document apparently involved in a purchase of foreign crude drugs at the time of the 1937 audit was a letter requesting quotations on five different products. Similar letters were supposedly sent to all five Canadian vendors"；> "McGloon, the comptroller, testified that he was not aware of it prior to December 10, 1938, when the carbon copies of requests for quotations, original replies and schedules were first discovered in the files of Robert Dietrich."
  - 应收地域分布（Section III 印刷页 97—98）：1937-12-31 外国 crude drug 应收账款按国别列示——England、Scotland、Wales、Ireland、Australia、British Guiana、New Zealand、India；证词称货款 > "if they were past due it was just a few days of the next month that they ran over, not any longer period."
  - 斯坦福废弃办公室与印刷源头（Section II 第 17 页）：> "to Stamford, Conn., where they located an abandoned office, which the Musicas had used as headquarters for preparing their fictitious documents. ### S008｜AIA 1939-03-04 事实通报

- 背景：AIA 执行委员会应会员要求，由特别委员会（Broad、Coates、Hurdman）据 SEC 听证记录（1939-01-05 起）整理事实摘要，密送全体会员，并声明不对审计师是否尽责表态。
- 原文摘录：> "The balance-sheet of McKesson & Robbins, Inc., as of December 31, 1937, showed among other assets customers' accounts receivable of $25,791,604.19, and inventories of $44,254,735.70, which now appear to have been overstated in total by about $19,000,000. Apparently about 10,000,000 of inventories, and about $9,000,000 of accounts receivable were nonexistent."
- 原文摘录（共谋结构）：> "the president of the corporation and the assistant treasurer (who under the president had charge of the purchase of crude drugs) and the head of the production and stock department, three brothers, with another brother on the outside, all under assumed names, were acting in collusion"。
- 原文摘录（Smith 担保与伪造文件）：> "W. W. Smith & Co., guaranteed McKesson & Robbins against losses on accounts receivable up to $900,000…the contract, the guarantee, and the credit report lacked substance or were forgeries."
- 原文摘录（供应商确认函）：> "they received by mail direct to their own offices, confirmations from these suppliers showing the amount of inventory held by them owned by McKesson & Robbins at the year end. These confirmations now appear to have been forged."
- 原文摘录（不函证的原因）：> "The accounts receivable were not confirmed by direct correspondence with debtors in this or any other department. The auditors testified that they discussed the question of circularization with the president and the president requested that circularization be not undertaken."
- 原文摘录（工作量与客户数）：> "The record shows that the entire 1937 audit, including branches, resulted in expenditure of 21,000 hours of chargeable time by the auditors."；虚构部门客户 > "more than 700 in number at December 31, 1937"；> "there were no bad-debt losses or allowances to customers in connection with the foreign crude-drug business."
- 支持主张：C004、C005、C009。
- 局限：AIA 内部通报，转述听证证词；引语均系"审计师作证称"，不构成对事实的独立确认。
- 版权状态：行业档案，仅存最小摘录与链接。
